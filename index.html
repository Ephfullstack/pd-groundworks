Is resin bound more expensive than block paving or tarmac?
Not necessarily. Where we can apply an overlay system to your existing surface, resin can be more cost-effective than a full dig-out and relay. We assess every site individually and provide a clear, honest comparison during your free survey.
How long does installation typically take?
Most residential driveways are completed within 1–3 days depending on size and complexity. Patios and pathways are often finished within a single day. We provide a precise programme during your quotation so you can plan accordingly.
Do I need to prepare anything before the installation?
Very little is required from you. Simply clear the area of pots, furniture and personal items. We handle all groundwork, preparation and clean-up. We will need access to water and electricity on the day — your project manager will confirm all details in advance.
Can resin be laid over existing block paving or flagstones?
No. Resin bound surfacing must be laid over a solid, stable, properly prepared base to carry our workmanship guarantee. Installing over block paving or flags risks surface failure over time, and the outline of the flags may become visible. We assess your base at the free survey stage.
What guarantee do you provide?
All installations carry a written workmanship guarantee. We use only premium materials from established UK suppliers, and our team is trained to the highest industry standards. Full documentation is provided at handover.
